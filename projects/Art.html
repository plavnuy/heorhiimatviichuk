Visual Works

Author Heorhii Matviichuk
Medium Painting, Digital Art, Illustration
Period 2015 — Present

Overview

This is a personal visual archive that brings together abstract paintings, cyberpunk-inspired works, and experimental illustrations.

The projects are not tied to a single style or medium. Each series reflects a different state, rhythm, or internal landscape explored through visual form.

The focus is on atmosphere, intuition, and visual tension rather than narrative or explanation.

Abstract Paintings

Abstract works explore color, movement, and balance. These paintings are driven by intuition and physical gesture, often emerging without a predefined concept.

Cyberpunk & Digital Visions

Cyberpunk works focus on urban density, technological tension, and fragmented identities.

Neon light, contrast, and sharp forms create a sense of future that feels unstable and alive.

Illustrations & Experiments

This section includes visual experiments, illustrations, and sketches created across different periods.

Some works are standalone ideas, others are fragments of larger conceptual explorations.

Context

These works exist outside commercial constraints and are part of an ongoing personal practice.

The archive grows organically, reflecting shifts in perception, tools, and internal focus over time.

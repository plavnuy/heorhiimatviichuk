Client Dr. Gavrylin
Role Creative Development, Animation, Front-End
Year 2024

Website

Dr. Gavrylin is a YouTube blogger exploring psychology, altered states of consciousness, and the cultural context of psychedelics. Alongside video content, he writes long-form reflective texts about perception, life, and inner experience. These texts became the foundation of the website.

The goal was to create a digital space closer to reading a personal notebook than browsing a conventional blog — calm, focused, and free from algorithmic pressure.

I designed and implemented a custom animated hero section that sets the emotional tone from the first interaction. The animation acts as an entry point into the author’s worldview, intentionally slowing the user down and shifting attention inward.

The project was built using a vibe coding approach, prioritizing rhythm, atmosphere, and intuition over rigid UI patterns. I handled the full front-end layout and implementation, with particular attention to typography, spacing, and long-form readability.

The mobile version was designed and implemented with the same care, preserving reading rhythm, visual balance, and lightweight animations adapted for smaller screens.

Sleep Supplement — Packaging & Promotion

As an extension of the project, Dr. Gavrylin released a sleep supplement.

I designed the packaging label and promotional visuals, translating the website’s visual language into a physical product and marketing materials.

Outcome

The website became a focused platform for thoughtful reading rather than passive content consumption.

For me, this project was an exploration of how design, animation, and code can support introspection and meaning — not just information delivery.

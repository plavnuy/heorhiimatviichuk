Dr. Gavrylin

Client Dr. Gavrylin
Role Creative Development, Animation, Front-End
Year 2024

Website

Dr. Gavrylin is a YouTube blogger exploring psychology, altered states of consciousness, and the cultural context of psychedelics. Beyond video content, he writes long-form texts reflecting on life, perception, and inner experience. These texts became the core of the website.

The goal was to create a digital space that feels closer to reading a personal notebook than browsing a typical blog or media platform — calm, focused, and free from algorithmic pressure.

I designed and implemented a custom animated hero section that sets the emotional tone from the first interaction. The animation is not decorative; it works as an entry point into the author’s worldview, slowing the user down and shifting attention inward.

The project was built using a vibe coding approach, prioritizing rhythm, atmosphere, and intuition over rigid UI patterns. I handled the full front-end layout and implementation, focusing on typography, spacing, and long-form readability.

The mobile version was designed and implemented with the same care — preserving visual balance, reading rhythm, and lightweight animations adapted specifically for smaller screens.

Sleep Supplement — Packaging & Promotion

As an extension of the project, Dr. Gavrylin released a sleep supplement.

I designed the packaging label and promotional visuals, translating the same visual language from the website into a physical product and advertising materials.

Outcome

The website became a focused platform for thoughtful reading rather than passive consumption.

For me, this project was an exploration of how design, animation, and code can support introspection and meaning — not just content delivery.

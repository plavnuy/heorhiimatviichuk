Support Visuals

Focus Fundraising & Military Support
Role Graphic Design, Visual Communication
Context Ukraine, Wartime

Overview

This project brings together visual materials created to support military fundraising initiatives and a custom patch designed for Ukrainian defenders.

The primary goal was clarity and emotional precision — visuals that communicate urgency and solidarity without exaggeration or aestheticization of violence.

Fundraising Visuals

I created graphic assets for fundraising campaigns, used across social platforms and private channels.

The visuals were designed to be direct, readable, and adaptable — functioning at different scales and in fast-moving information environments.

Patch Design

As part of this work, I designed a patch intended for real-world use by military personnel.

The design focuses on symbolism, durability, and clear form — suitable for embroidery and physical production.

Design Principles

Working within a wartime context requires restraint and responsibility.

These visuals were created to serve a function — to help, to support, and to communicate — rather than to exist as standalone artworks.

Reflection

This work represents a different role of design — one rooted in necessity rather than expression.

In moments like these, design becomes a quiet tool of support and coordination.

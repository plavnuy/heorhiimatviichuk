Support Visuals

Focus Fundraising & Military Support
Role Graphic Design, Visual Communication
Context Ukraine, Wartime

Overview

This project brings together visual materials created to support military fundraising initiatives and a custom patch designed for Ukrainian defenders.

Fundraising Visuals

I created graphic assets for fundraising campaigns, used across social platforms and private channels.

Patch Design

As part of this work, I designed a patch intended for real-world use by military personnel.

The design focuses on symbolism, durability, and clear form — suitable for embroidery and physical production.

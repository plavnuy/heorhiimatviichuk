Role & Partnership

The partnership with LOCATED began in 2024 with the development of custom merchandise and continues to this day. My role includes concept development, ideation and brainstorming, graphic design, as well as prepress preparation of technically complex layouts — including full-color silkscreen printing.

Over time, this collaboration evolved into a broader design partnership. I work with the project across multiple areas: visual consulting, creative brainstorming, material design, and the development of art-direction solutions for various brand collaborations.

located × Vartis

Vartis — a major metal holding company in Ukraine — approached LOCATED with the task of creating contemporary custom merchandise for retail sale. During the process, a wide range of design concepts was developed within Vartis’ corporate visual language.

As a result, six branded T-shirts were approved and produced. A key conceptual outcome of the project was the phrase “The future is iron”. The phrase expresses belief in the future while simultaneously asserting its technological nature, functioning as a metaphor and a direct reference to the company’s core product — metal.

This phrase later became a core brand message for a larger Vartis advertising campaign and was subsequently used in promotional videos and billboard communications.

located × AMO

T-shirts designed for employees and distributed in exchange for donations during a fundraising campaign that raised 540,000 UAH for drones supporting soldiers deployed in the Kramatorsk direction.

The graphic concept combines an abstract drone and a bird, layered with a topographic map and precise coordinates. The design reflects both technological presence and symbolic protection, connecting the visual language of the garment with its humanitarian purpose.

located × Hram

A collaboration between Hram bar and popular blogger Yasyushka. My contribution included graphic design, retouching, color correction, and the visual realization of the client’s idea.

I was also responsible for prepress preparation for silkscreen printing and quality control of the production process, working closely with LOCATED founder Yura to ensure consistency and execution quality.

Logo & Identity System

The logo development began with research into historical Ukrainian typography and graphic traditions, aiming to preserve cultural roots while establishing a contemporary visual language.

During archival research, a stylized letter "K" was discovered whose form naturally suggested the numerals "1" and "9." This discovery formed the conceptual foundation—merging letter and numbers into a unified graphic symbol that represents both name and location.

The resulting mark functions as both wordmark and symbolic identifier, adaptable across various applications from digital interfaces to physical exhibition materials.

Exhibition: Transformation. Roma Dase

Roma Dase's first solo exhibition marked a significant transition from tattoo culture into contemporary art practice. The exhibition explored abstraction, form, and color as primary visual languages.

"Transformation" functioned as both theme and process—documenting the artist's journey through transition, exploration, and the materialization of internal visions into tangible artistic expressions.

The exhibition design created deliberate spaces for discovery, allowing viewers to experience the threshold between artistic disciplines and witness the emergence of a new creative voice.

Exhibition: Liminal

"Liminal" explored transitional states—the threshold between known and unknown where design becomes an instrument for transformation and creation.

The exhibition focused on design thinking principles and the creative process itself, revealing typically hidden elements: prototypes, material studies, sketches, and production stages.

Objects were presented in moments of becoming—as ideas, explorations of form, and studies in materiality. This approach invited viewers into the creative process rather than simply presenting finished works.

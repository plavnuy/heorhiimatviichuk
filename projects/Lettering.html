Overview

This series documents my personal experiments with lettering, focusing on two main directions: the bold, expressive "Thug Life" studies, and explorations of the word "Kyiv / Київ" in digital and hand-drawn forms.

The goal is to explore gesture, form, rhythm, and the balance between digital precision and human imperfection, rather than creating finished typography.

Many works exist between drawing and type, capturing motion, pressure, and spontaneity, often starting without a fixed concept.

Process

Each piece begins as a quick sketch or intuitive gesture. "Thug Life" lettering experiments focus on expressive shapes, high contrast strokes, and motion dynamics.

"Kyiv / Київ" studies explore letter structure, balance, and visual rhythm, combining digital refinement with hand-drawn textures.

Some experiments are polished digitally, while others remain raw and unresolved intentionally, allowing form to emerge naturally without constraints of usability or branding.

Selected Works

Reflection

These experiments inform my approach to branding, logo design, and visual systems.

Lettering becomes a way to explore form, motion, and balance before committing to structured typographic solutions.

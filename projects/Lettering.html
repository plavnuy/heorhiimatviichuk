Lettering Experiments

Focus Form, Rhythm, Gesture
Type Personal Research
Medium Digital & Hand-drawn

Overview

This series documents my ongoing experiments with lettering as a form of visual research rather than finished typography.

The focus is on exploring shape, balance, pressure, and negative space — often starting without a fixed concept.

Many of these works exist between drawing and type, capturing motion and imperfection.

Process

Each piece begins as a quick sketch or intuitive gesture.

Some experiments are refined digitally, others remain raw and unresolved by intention.

This approach allows form to emerge naturally, without constraints of usability or branding.

Selected Works

Reflection

These experiments influence my approach to branding, logo design, and visual systems.

Lettering becomes a way to think through form before committing to structure.

Raconteur Studio

Client Indy Hait
Role Typography, Animation, Front-End Development
Year 2025

Overview

Raconteur Studio is the creative hub of Indy Hait, a renowned Ukrainian director with a rich portfolio of music videos for both Ukrainian and international artists.

The goal was to create a minimal, elegant landing page that reflects the studio’s professionalism while giving space to subtle motion and typography as visual identity.

Typography & Animation

I developed the typographic system and simple animations for the landing page to create a clean but dynamic first impression.

Animations were kept minimal, emphasizing interaction and rhythm rather than flashy effects, letting the content speak for itself.

Outcome

The final landing page is minimalistic, fast, and elegant, reflecting the studio’s professional image while providing a subtle animated experience.

It works as a digital business card for the studio and a showcase of Indy Hait’s visual style in a concise, refined format.

Sayenko & Kharenko

Project Logo Concept
Role Brand / Logo Design
Status Concept Proposal (Not Selected)

Overview

Sayenko & Kharenko is a leading Ukrainian law firm with a strong international reputation and a full-service legal practice.

This project represents a conceptual logo proposal developed as part of an identity exploration for the firm.

Although the concept was not ultimately adopted, it reflects a clear strategic and symbolic approach to legal branding.

Brand Context

The firm operates at the intersection of Ukrainian law and international legal standards.

The visual identity therefore required a balance between authority, clarity, and restrained modernity — avoiding decorative or overly expressive solutions.

Concept

The core idea of the symbol is based on the paragraph sign (§), a universal visual reference strongly associated with law and legal structure.

The paragraph form was reconstructed using the first letters of the firm’s name — S and K.

This approach merges legal symbolism with the personal identity of the firm’s founders, resulting in a mark that is both abstract and meaningful.

Symbol Construction

The symbol is built through strict geometric logic, ensuring visual balance, symmetry, and legibility at different scales.

The construction avoids literal illustration, favoring an intellectual reading appropriate for a high-profile legal brand.

Reflection

Even though the concept was not selected, it demonstrates a strategic design process rooted in meaning, symbolism, and brand positioning.

For me, this project was an exercise in translating complex institutional values into a single, precise mark.

Such explorations often remain unseen, yet they form an essential part of professional design practice.

Yoga Teacher Website — Design & Development

Carly Snedeker is a Zurich-based yoga teacher focused on mindful movement, presence, and long-term personal practice.

The goal of the project was to create a calm, confident, and timeless digital presence — a landing page that reflects the atmosphere of her classes rather than following typical wellness clichés.

The website serves as a central point of contact, schedule reference, and introduction to Carly’s approach to yoga.

Visual System & Typography

I developed a unified visual language for the page, including typographic decisions, spacing, rhythm, and overall tone.

The design avoids decorative excess and instead focuses on clarity, air, and subtle modernist balance — allowing content and photography to breathe.

Landing Page

I handled the full layout and front-end implementation, ensuring responsiveness, performance, and long-term maintainability.

In addition to design and development, I set up Google indexing and basic SEO foundations to ensure the site is discoverable and correctly represented in search results.

I continue to support the project by monitoring updates, adjusting content when needed, and maintaining visual consistency as the practice evolves.

Printed Flyer

Alongside the website, I designed a printed flyer in a modern art–influenced style.

The flyer extends the same visual language into physical space, reinforcing recognition and coherence between online and offline touchpoints.

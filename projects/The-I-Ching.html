I Ching Digital Oracle

Type Personal Mini Product
Role Concept, UX, UI, Development
Year 2024

Overview

While exploring Carl Jung’s ideas on synchronicity, I discovered the ancient practice of divination using the I Ching — the Book of Changes.

In the digital world, I couldn’t find a simple, calm, and non-overloaded interpretation of this ritual. Most existing tools felt either too mystical or too mechanical.

This project is my attempt to translate the essence of the ritual into a minimal digital experience — a small product made primarily for personal use, later shared with friends and close circles.

Concept & Ritual

The interaction is intentionally simple and tactile. The user taps the screen six times.

Each tap generates either one or two lines, forming a hexagram step by step — an abstract digital reflection of the traditional yarrow stick or coin method.

Once the hexagram is complete, the user receives a short interpretation from the I Ching — not as a prediction, but as a prompt for reflection during moments of uncertainty.

Interface & Visual Language

The interface avoids decoration and symbolism overload. Visual restraint helps preserve the feeling of a quiet ritual rather than a game or fortune-telling app.

Outcome

The project became a small but meaningful tool for myself and others. Friends described it not as fortune-telling, but as a way to pause, externalize inner questions, and reframe uncertainty.

It also served as a personal experiment in vibe coding — designing not for metrics or scalability, but for atmosphere, rhythm, and psychological resonance.

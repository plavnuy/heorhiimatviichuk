Overview

Vognyar is a natural Ukrainian hot sauce brand built around deep respect for raw ingredients, farming culture, and the craft of chili cultivation. The brand has been present on the market since 2017 and continues to be sold in retail stores across Ukraine.

I developed the logo, label system, and packaging design that has remained visually consistent since its launch — proving long-term viability both aesthetically and in production.

Beyond visual identity, the project includes packaging engineering, retail logic, and a scalable system for future product lines.

Label System & Bottles

The label design has remained unchanged since 2017, demonstrating a rare level of stability in food branding.

The system supports multiple sauce variations while maintaining a unified shelf presence and clear differentiation through color and hierarchy.

Packaging & Production Logic

A key task was designing transport and retail boxes that are easy to produce, assemble, and secure without additional tooling.

The tamper-evident solution is integrated directly into the box construction, ensuring control of first opening while remaining cost-efficient.


When multiple boxes are placed side by side and correctly oriented, the logo visually connects into a single large mark — enhancing shelf impact without additional print costs.

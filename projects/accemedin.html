Accemedin

Client Accemedin — Ukrainian Medical Education Platform
Role UX/UI Designer — Web & Mobile
Year 2016

Overview

Accemedin is an educational online platform created exclusively for Ukrainian healthcare professionals.

The platform allows doctors to take interactive courses, earn scores, and continuously improve their professional skills. International educational models were adapted specifically for modern Ukrainian medical practice.

This was my first professional experience in web design.

Interface & UX Design

The challenge was to make complex educational content intuitive and clear for professionals.

I focused on usability, clear information hierarchy, and responsive layouts for both web and mobile platforms.

Outcome

The platform continues to operate with the original design, serving a large audience of healthcare professionals in Ukraine.

This project gave me my first deep experience in UX/UI design, including responsive design and interfaces for interactive education.

DOBRO

Project Naming & Logo Design
Product Project & Resource Management Tool
Role Naming, Brand Strategy, Logo Design
Year 2025

Background & Challenge

The product initially carried the working name PRM, a functional abbreviation for project resource management. While descriptive, it lacked emotional resonance and memorability.

The system helps managers plan and control project budgets and labor costs, predict deviations from planned metrics, and build a structured view of company resources.

Despite its functional strength, the product name felt technical, generic, and difficult to remember — especially in a competitive SaaS landscape. The goal was to move away from “Excel + Jira plugins” associations and position the product as a single, human-centered management tool.

The ideal client is a small to medium business manager aged 28–45, overseeing multiple projects and large resource pools. They often struggle with missed deadlines, budget overruns, and the complexity of planning and coordinating execution across teams.

Naming & Exploration

The new name needed to be easy to read and pronounce, memorable, and aligned with modern SaaS naming trends. It should convey trust, clarity, and support, reinforcing the idea of an assistant that helps managers regain control.

A broad brainstorming phase explored functional, emotional, and metaphorical directions — from predictive and analytical concepts to colloquial and human-centered names. Competitor analysis revealed a dominance of short, abstract, and neutral SaaS names, presenting an opportunity to stand out with a warmer, more meaningful identity.

Final Choice

Stakeholders shortlisted three naming options: Fasto, DOBRO, and PMKit.

For each option, logo concepts were developed and tested in various contexts. DOBRO was chosen for its emotional clarity, memorability, and strong associative meaning.

Outcome

DOBRO became a human, confident, and supportive brand, contrasting the cold and technical tone of many project management tools.

The naming and logo system laid a foundation for further brand development, allowing the product to communicate trust, care, and control through a simple identity.

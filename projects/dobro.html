DOBRO

Project Naming & Logo Design
Product Project & Resource Management Tool
Role Naming, Brand Strategy, Logo Design
Year 2025

Background

The product originally had a working name PRM — a functional abbreviation that described project resource management but lacked emotional connection and memorability.

The system helps managers plan and control project budgets and labor costs, predict deviations from planned metrics and build a structured view of company resources.

Problem

While functionally strong, the product name did not support positioning or brand perception. It felt technical, generic and difficult to remember — especially in a competitive SaaS environment.

The goal was to move away from “Excel + Jira plugins” associations and position the product as a single, clear and human-centered management tool.

Target Audience

The ideal client is a small to medium business manager aged 28–45, managing many projects and large resource pools.

They struggle with missed deadlines, budget overruns and the complexity of planning and controlling execution across teams.

Naming Criteria

The new name had to be easy to read and pronounce, memorable, and aligned with modern SaaS naming trends.

It should reflect trust, clarity and support, reinforcing the idea of an assistant that helps managers regain control.

Exploration

A broad brainstorming phase explored functional, emotional and metaphorical directions — from predictive and analytical concepts to colloquial and human-centered names.

Competitor analysis revealed a dominance of short, abstract and neutral SaaS names, creating an opportunity to stand out with a warmer and more meaningful identity.

Final Choice

Stakeholders shortlisted three naming options: Fasto, DOBRO and PMKit.

For each option, logo concepts were developed and tested in different contexts. The name DOBRO was selected for its emotional clarity, memorability and strong associative meaning.

Outcome

DOBRO became a human, confident and supportive brand, contrasting the cold and technical tone of many project management tools.

The naming and logo system laid a foundation for further brand development, allowing the product to communicate trust, care and control through a simple identity.

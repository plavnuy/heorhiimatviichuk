PRODJ Mobile App

Project E-commerce mobile application
Role UX-UI Design
Format Educational Case Study
Year 2017

Overview

PRODJ is a network of professional DJ equipment stores with more than 10 years on the Ukrainian market. The company operates three offline stores in Kyiv, Kharkiv and Odesa, offering DJ gear, sound and light equipment, musical instruments, as well as rental, installation and service.

This case study explores the design of a mobile e-commerce application aimed at simplifying the purchase, selection and discovery of professional DJ equipment for users with different levels of expertise.

Users & Personas

UX Goals

The application aims to balance professional depth with accessibility: helping experienced DJs quickly find specific gear, while guiding beginners through curated selections, comparisons and educational content.

Key goals include fast product discovery, transparent pricing, trust through expert recommendations and seamless mobile checkout.

Outcome

The result is a conceptual mobile app design that reflects the professional nature of the brand while remaining approachable for newcomers.

This educational case demonstrates product thinking, UX research synthesis and interface design tailored to a niche e-commerce domain with high user expectations.

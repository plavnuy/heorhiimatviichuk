EasyMeets

Project Scheduling Web Platform
Focus UX/UI & Product Design
Design Process 7 weeks
Role Research, UX Architecture, UI Design, Prototyping

Overview

EasyMeets is a comprehensive scheduling platform designed to simplify availability tracking and meeting organization for both internal teams and external collaborators.

The product focuses on seamless calendar management, effortless sharing of availability, automated event creation, and timely reminders.

Research & Analysis

Scheduling meetings is often inefficient due to scattered calendars, constant back-and-forth communication, and poor coordination between internal teams and external participants.

User Interviews

Users experience endless back-and-forth just to agree on a meeting time.

Conflicts arise when managing multiple calendars across work and personal life.

Coordination becomes difficult when participants use different tools.

Group scheduling across teams and time zones quickly becomes overwhelming.

Paid meetings and consultations require extra tools and manual follow-ups.

Competitor Analysis

Research identified baseline expectations for modern scheduling platforms.

Multi-calendar sync with Google, Outlook, and iCloud is mandatory.

Group scheduling tools significantly reduce coordination effort.

Customization of buffers, rules, and meeting types is essential.

Payment integrations are critical for consultants and paid sessions.

Automatic time zone handling prevents scheduling errors.

Ideation

Ideation focused on shifting from time-slot negotiation to transparent availability sharing.

Concepts emphasized automation, clarity, and reduced cognitive load.

Architecture & Wireframes

Information architecture supports fast access to calendars, events, availability, and scheduling rules.

Wireframes emphasize clear hierarchy and predictable navigation.

Components & UI

A modular UI component system supports scalability across dashboards, event pages, and settings.

The visual language balances clarity and warmth for professional use.

Prototype

An interactive prototype validated the full scheduling flow, from availability setup to meeting confirmation.

Early testing showed reduced friction compared to traditional workflows.

Outcome

EasyMeets resulted in a clear and scalable scheduling platform ready for further product development.

The case demonstrates structured UX thinking, competitive analysis, and end-to-end product design execution.

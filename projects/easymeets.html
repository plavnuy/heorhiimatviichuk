EasyMeets

Project Scheduling Web Platform
Focus UX/UI & Product Design
Design Process 7 weeks
Role Research, UX Architecture, UI Design, Prototyping

Overview

EasyMeets is a comprehensive scheduling platform designed to simplify tracking availability and organizing meetings with both internal teams and external collaborators.

The product focuses on seamless calendar management, effortless sharing of availability, automated event creation and timely reminders.

Research & Analysis

Scheduling meetings is often time-consuming and inefficient due to scattered calendars, back-and-forth communication and lack of seamless coordination between internal teams and external participants.

User Interviews

Scheduling meetings is often time-consuming and inefficient due to scattered calendars, back-and-forth communication, and the lack of seamless coordination between internal teams and external participants.

Users experience endless back-and-forth conversations just to find a suitable meeting time.

Conflicts frequently arise when managing multiple calendars, including work, personal, and shared schedules.

Coordinating with people who use different tools or are unwilling to share their availability is difficult and frustrating.

Managing group availability across large teams or multiple time zones quickly becomes overwhelming.

Handling payments for paid meetings or consultations requires extra effort, manual follow-ups, and additional tools.

Competitors Analysis

Research highlighted several must-have expectations in modern scheduling platforms.

Seamless multi-calendar synchronization with Google, Outlook and iCloud is considered a baseline feature.

Group scheduling tools such as round-robin, polls and shared availability significantly reduce coordination effort.

Advanced customization options for buffers, scheduling rules and meeting types add flexibility for professional use.

Payment integrations via Stripe or PayPal are essential for consultants, coaches and paid events.

Automated time zone handling is critical for international teams to avoid confusion and scheduling errors.

Ideation

The ideation phase focused on simplifying the mental model of scheduling by shifting from time-slot negotiation to availability sharing.

Concepts were explored around transparency, automation and reducing cognitive load for both organizers and participants.

Architecture & Wireframes

Information architecture was designed to support quick access to calendars, events, availability and scheduling rules.

Wireframes focused on clear hierarchy, predictable navigation and progressive disclosure of complex options.

Components & UI

A modular component system was created to support scalability and consistency across dashboards, event pages and settings.

The visual language balances clarity and warmth, ensuring the platform feels professional without being rigid or overwhelming.

Prototype

An interactive prototype validated the end-to-end scheduling flow, from availability setup to meeting confirmation and reminders.

Early feedback confirmed reduced friction and faster scheduling compared to traditional calendar-based workflows.

Outcome

EasyMeets resulted in a clear, scalable scheduling platform ready for further product development and technical implementation.

The case demonstrates structured UX thinking, competitive analysis and end-to-end product design execution.

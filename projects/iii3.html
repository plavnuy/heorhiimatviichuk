Overview

III3 is an underground Ukrainian rap album by ron4ik x mxtixn, written and released during the war.

The project confronts the immediacy of life, identity, and struggle, expressed through a raw and direct musical language.

The visual concept and album cover were designed to resonate with this emotional and cultural landscape.

Visual Concept

The cover concept is rooted in mood, rhythm, and expressive tension. It reflects both the internal world of the artists and the external conditions that shaped the music.

The imagery, typography, and composition were designed to complement the sonic tone — immediate, gritty, and unfiltered.

Reflection

This project is a collaboration rooted in trust, intensity, and cultural context.

Designing for music that emerges from lived experience — especially in times of upheaval — challenges the designer to work with honesty and clarity rather than polish.

Jernov Jewellery

Project Brochure Design
Role Graphic Design / Editorial Layout
Format Printed Brochure

Overview & Design Approach

Jernov Jewellery is a brand centered on refined form, material integrity, and timeless aesthetics.

The brochure was designed as an editorial object, inspired by art books and fashion publications. Layout, rhythm, and white space communicate the brand’s philosophy without decorative distraction.

Typography serves a structural role, while a strict grid system and generous margins create a calm, precise, and luxurious reading experience.

Result

The brochure functions as a physical extension of the brand, communicating its values through form and restraint.

It is intended for client presentations, showrooms, and private brand communications.

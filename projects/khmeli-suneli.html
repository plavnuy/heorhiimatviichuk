Khmeli-Suneli

Client Khmeli-Suneli Restaurant
Role Logo Design
Year 2015

Overview

Khmeli-Suneli is a Georgian restaurant located in the center of Kyiv, inspired by the warmth, hospitality, and rich culinary traditions of Georgia.

The restaurant has been operating successfully for many years and continues to welcome guests, preserving its original identity.

I designed the logo in 2015, focusing on cultural authenticity, warmth, and a timeless visual language.

Logo Concept

The upper part of the logo is written in the Georgian alphabet and drawn by hand, emphasizing authenticity and a human touch.

Handwritten lettering reflects Georgian hospitality — warm, generous, and personal — rather than polished or corporate.

The composition balances tradition with clarity, allowing the logo to remain readable and expressive across signage, menus, and interior elements.

Cultural Context

“Khmeli-Suneli” is more than a spice blend — it is a symbol of Georgian cuisine and a metaphor for richness, warmth, and generosity.

The visual identity supports this idea by referencing traditional scripts and organic forms rather than modern minimalism.

This approach helps the restaurant feel authentic and rooted, even as trends in restaurant branding change over time.

Restaurant Experience

The restaurant offers a wide selection of traditional Georgian dishes — from pkhali, kharcho, and khachapuri to kebabs and baklava.

The atmosphere combines modern interior design with a strong cultural spirit, supported by friendly staff and a welcoming environment.

The logo plays a key role in shaping first impressions, setting expectations of warmth and generosity even before entering.

Longevity

One of the most important outcomes of this project is its durability over time.

Designed in 2015, the logo is still in use today, proving that culturally grounded design can outlive short-term visual trends.

For me, this project is a reminder that strong identity is built on meaning, not decoration.

Mitus

Project Startup Product Design
Domain Farm Transition Planning
Role Product Design, UX/UI, Branding
Year 2024–2025

Overview

Mitus is a startup platform designed to help Australian farmers plan and manage generational farm transitions — covering ownership, management, financial, legal and operational continuity.

Farm transition planning is one of the most complex and emotionally charged processes in agriculture. It typically involves multiple stakeholders: farmers, family members, advisors, lawyers and financial institutions. Information is scattered across documents, spreadsheets and conversations, making progress difficult to track and decisions hard to align.

Mitus aims to become a single digital workspace where transition plans, documents, tasks and conversations are structured and visible. The product combines planning tools, questionnaires, dashboards and CRM-like data structures into one coherent system — bringing clarity, alignment and long-term confidence to the transition process.

Branding & Logo

Three logo directions were explored to balance trust, clarity and a modern technology feel suitable for conservative industries.

The final direction focuses on calm confidence and long-term thinking, avoiding aggressive or overly “startup-like” aesthetics.

Interface Design

The interface design is inspired by CRM systems, with an emphasis on dashboards, structured tables and clear information hierarchy.

Key modules include transition overviews, client profiles, questionnaires, document management and progress tracking.

UX Approach

The UX prioritizes clarity over density, helping non-technical users navigate complex information without feeling overwhelmed.

Visual structure, progressive disclosure and familiar CRM patterns reduce cognitive load and build trust in the system.

Outcome

Mitus evolved into a structured, scalable product foundation suitable for advisors, relationship managers and farmers alike.

The case demonstrates end-to-end product thinking — from brand identity to complex interface systems in a real-world startup context.

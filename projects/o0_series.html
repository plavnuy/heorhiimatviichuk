Overview

o0series is an interdisciplinary art project that brings together Ukrainian visual artists and electronic musicians to create a series of hand-painted Dino sculptures accompanied by live audiovisual performances.

The project was initiated spontaneously, starting from a request to design a website. This moment became the starting point of my path into creative coding and web-based experimentation, which later expanded into a full-scale visual system.

I acted as a creative partner and designer, responsible for identity, logo development, website vibe-coding, 3D integration, web animation, motion design, social media visuals, and conceptual framing of Dino as the project’s central symbol.

Dino & Symbolism

Dino is the mascot and talisman of 20ft Radio. Before its visual form and logo were developed, I conducted an in-depth symbolic discovery focused on reptilian imagery, cyclical rhythms, and loop-based musical structures. The dinosaur emerged as a metaphor for an ancient, instinctive force — similar to the ouroboros cycle — embedded in repetitive sound, bodily response, and visual rhythm.

From this research, Dino was positioned as a portal to childhood: a state of play, curiosity, and free experimentation, where creativity operates intuitively rather than rationally. The figurine became a symbolic gateway between primal instinct and playful innocence, forming the myth-narrative foundation of the project.


Only after this narrative was defined did the logo development begin. Over time, the concept was intentionally simplified, allowing Dino to function as an intuitive, approachable figure. In o0series, it becomes a canvas — reinterpreted by artists visually and expanded by musicians through sound, forming temporary yet complete audiovisual identities.

Logo Development

The o0series logo was developed as a flexible, modular mark rather than a fixed symbol. It reflects the dual nature of the project: structure and experimentation, repetition and mutation.


The form references zeros as both origin points and cycles, while remaining neutral enough to coexist with highly expressive artworks and diverse musical aesthetics.

Website

The o0series website became the starting point of the entire project and my entry into creative coding. It was developed not as a traditional promotional platform, but as an experiential digital space.


The o0series website became the starting point of the entire project and my entry into creative coding. It was developed not as a traditional promotional platform, but as an experiential digital space.

I built the site using a vibe-coding approach, prioritizing rhythm, atmosphere, and interaction over rigid structure. The interface functions as an extension of the project’s philosophy — slow, tactile, and slightly unstable.

The website integrates 3D elements, web-based animation, and custom interactions to blur the boundary between documentation and artistic environment. It acts as a living archive of the project, hosting videos, interviews, and process materials.

Rather than explaining o0series directly, the site invites the viewer to explore and feel the project through motion, sound, and visual tension.

Posters

Instagram functions as the project’s primary public interface. It connects physical objects, live events, and digital storytelling into a single narrative stream.

I developed post templates, motion elements, and visual rhythm for the feed, balancing documentation with atmosphere. The goal was not promotion, but immersion — allowing the audience to gradually enter the world of o0series.

Collaboration with 22f

These elements extend the identity into the physical world, reinforcing the idea of each Dino as a unique art object rather than a decorative artifact.

Boxes & Stencils

Each Dino sculpture is packaged in a custom box developed in collaboration with 22F Studio. The packaging includes stencil graphics and structural solutions that emphasize the object’s collectible status.

The box functions as both protection and narrative frame — an interface between the sculpture, the artist, and the future owner.

Events, Artists & Musicians

The project includes nine visual artists and nine electronic musicians, paired based on shared sensibility, temperament, and aesthetic language.

Each pair culminates in a live event where the sculpture is presented alongside a specially created musical performance. Events take place approximately once per month.

The first collaboration featured artist Valentуne Metуk and producer, composer, and sound designer Emil Asadow, whose semi-improvised audiovisual performance explored interaction with space and audience mood.

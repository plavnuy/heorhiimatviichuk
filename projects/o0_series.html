Overview

o0series is an art project uniting Ukrainian electronic musicians and visual artists to create hand-painted sculptures of the twenty-foot Dino mascot, accompanied by live music performances. The project highlights the collaboration between visual and musical artists, transforming each sculpture into an audiovisual experience.

My role included branding, web design and coding, 3D integration, motion animation, logo creation, identity development, social media visuals, and giving conceptual depth to Dino as the central figure of the project.

Project Concept

The project pairs nine visual artists with nine electronic musicians. Each artist decorates the Dino sculpture in their unique style, while the musician creates a corresponding live set, forming a complete audiovisual environment around the figure.

The completed sculptures receive custom packaging and certificates of authenticity designed by 22F Studio. Videos of the creation process and interviews with the artists are shared on the o0series website, while each pairing is celebrated with a musical event approximately once a month.

Artists & Musicians

Participating artists include D.Tsey, Dase, Krasivity, Masiks.Prod, Nick Broslavskiy, Prisya, Roma Broslavskiy, Sestry Feldman, and Valentуne Metуk. Musicians include 0b3th, Bogdan Zaiets, Elija, Gnatenko, Emil Asadow, Keay, Native Outsider, noorj, and tofudj.

The first collaboration featured artist Valentуne Metуk and musician Emil Asadow, presenting a semi-improvised audiovisual set engaging with the audience and space, enhanced by psychedelic visuals.

About Dino

Dino, the twenty-foot mascot of 20ft Radio, symbolizes a portal to childhood — a space for free expression, play, and inspiration. Gifted to the radio co-founder by DJ Oleg Baranovskiy, Dino travels with the team, appearing in live sets, broadcasts, and trips. Each artist’s intervention and the accompanying music set create a new identity for the iconic figure.

Events & Charity

The first Dino sculpture presentation and Emil Asadow’s performance took place on May 30 at Community Garden, accompanied by a “paleontological” set from electronic musician Anthony Junkoid.

Entry to all events is free-donation. 50% of proceeds go to the Kyiv Angels volunteer organization, while donations above 500 UAH allow participation in a raffle for a hand-painted Dino.

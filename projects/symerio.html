Overview

Symerio is a consulting company working in the field of data science, analytics, and complex digital systems.

The task was to develop a logo that reflects the nature of digital information and the structured logic behind data-driven decision making.

The identity needed to feel precise, intelligent, and system-oriented, without unnecessary visual noise.

Concept

At its most fundamental level, all digital information exists in the form of binary code — 0 and 1.

These symbols became the conceptual foundation of the logo. They also reference I / O (Input / Output), a core principle of computational systems and data processing.

This dual meaning allows the logo to communicate both technological depth and functional clarity.

Logo Construction

The mark is built using minimal geometric forms, directly inspired by the shapes of 0 and 1.

These elements are arranged to highlight the idea of information flowing into the system (input), being processed, and leaving as a result (output).

The resulting symbol is abstract yet readable, making it suitable for both technical and business contexts.

Outcome

The final logo provides Symerio with a strong, conceptual identity rooted in the fundamentals of computation.

It communicates precision, logic, and technical competence while remaining visually clean and timeless.

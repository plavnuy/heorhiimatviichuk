Trademotions

Product Trading Platform
Role Product Designer
Duration 13 months
Access Closed platform

Overview

Trademotions is a professional trading platform designed for fund managers and advanced traders. It provides real-time market data, pricing information, portfolio monitoring, and order management tools.

The platform enables users to track open positions, analyze profit and loss, and adjust trades in fast-moving market conditions.

I joined the project as a product designer and worked closely with the product manager and development team on improving usability, visual clarity, and overall product value.

Goal

The primary goal was to attract additional investments for further product development and enhance the platform’s value for end users.

This was achieved by improving the UI/UX of the interface and introducing new features aligned with real user needs.

Main Tasks

  • Communication with users and stakeholders
  • Providing structured tasks and requirements to developers
  • Analyzing feature ideas and proposing UI/UX solutions
  • Branding update and design system creation
>

Discovery

To understand the trading industry and the daily workflow of fund managers, I conducted deep primary research into the domain and existing product functionality.

The result was a set of mind maps and canvases that helped structure insights, identify pain points, and define opportunities for improvement.

Conceptualisation

User stories were systematized, and both new features and updates to existing functionality were ideated.

This process allowed the team to prioritize features that delivered the highest value by solving real, validated user problems.

Global Task List

  • Branding update and UI redesign
  • Header and side menu updates
  • Market View widget creation
  • Order Pad interface redesign
  • Portfolio widget creation
  • Conditional orders implementation

Additional Responsibilities

One of my responsibilities was creating Jira tickets for the development team, including detailed user stories and acceptance criteria.

This ensured a smooth transition from design to development and minimized miscommunication during implementation.

UI Redesign

Spacing & Grid

The main challenge was fitting extensive functionality into limited screen space. A 4px grid system was introduced to ensure precise alignment, consistency, and clear separation between widgets.

Colors

Color was used to establish hierarchy and guide attention. The palette consists of multiple shades of gray supported by accent colors. Red, as the brand’s primary color, was also used for errors and warnings to keep the system cohesive.

Typography — DIN Next Pro

  • High legibility at very small font sizes
  • Narrow letterforms optimized for dense interfaces
  • Monospaced numerals for financial data
  • Improved scanability in tables and columns

Light Theme, Responsiveness, Components

The light theme improves usability in bright environments while maintaining functional contrast. The platform is fully responsive, with modular and reusable components designed to adapt fluidly across screen sizes.

Orderpad updates

The Orderpad was redesigned to improve clarity, speed, and error prevention during trade execution. Input fields were reorganized to follow a logical trading flow, reducing friction and minimizing the risk of incorrect orders.

Clarity in Data Presentation

The "MarketView" widget embodies a design approach that emphasizes clarity, transforming complex market data into visually accessible insights. By using simplified layouts and strategically placed visuals, the platform minimizes cognitive load, allowing users to quickly grasp essential information. Every data point is presented with purpose, helping trade managers stay focused and confident in their interpretations.

Efficient Decision Support

Designed to support rapid, strategic decision-making, MarketView integrates real-time data and profitability indicators in an organized, digestible format. The use of intuitive navigation and segmented information enables users to monitor key trends and act swiftly on market changes. Each component serves a clear function, giving users a reliable foundation for making informed choices without unnecessary steps.

Cohesive User Experience

Throughout the platform, the MarketView widget maintains a consistent visual language that aligns with user-centered design principles. Color gradations, modular layouts, and clear typographic hierarchy create a seamless user journey from overview to detailed analysis. This cohesion ensures that users can navigate the platform effortlessly, accessing relevant insights and tools to make confident, well-supported trading decisions.

Market View

The Market View widget provides traders with a structured overview of real-time market data. Information is segmented into clear panels, allowing quick comparison of instruments, price movements, and performance indicators without cognitive overload.

Branding

The Trademotions sign is a combination of the T and M glyphs, symbolizing speed, dynamics, and continuous growth — core attributes of the platform and its users.

Outcome

The redesign improved clarity, reduced cognitive load, and created a scalable foundation for future feature development.

For me, this project was a deep dive into complex, data-dense product design, where precision, hierarchy, and restraint were as important as aesthetics.

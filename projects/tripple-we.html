Tripple We

Project Brand Identity
Role Brand Designer
Scope Logo, Visual System, Guidelines

Overview

Tripple We is a creative agency focused on supporting emerging talents — artists, creators, and cultural projects at the beginning of their journey.

The agency helps young creatives grow through strategic promotion, collaboration, and thoughtful execution of ideas.

My task was to design a brand identity that reflects unity, momentum, and the multiplying effect of collective creativity.

Brand Concept

The core idea behind Tripple We is multiplication — enhancing creative potential by combining people, ideas, and execution into a single system.

The name itself emphasizes collectivity: not “I”, but “We” — a shared creative force that grows stronger through collaboration.

The Glyph

The logo is a symbolic construction based on the word “WE”. The letter E is seamlessly hidden within the W, emphasizing unity and collective strength.

At the same time, the form visually resembles the Roman numeral III, referencing the idea of “triple” — growth through three reinforcing forces.

The shape also echoes a spring, symbolizing momentum, energy, and the upward movement that drives artists toward development and visibility.

Lockups & Usage

When used alongside other graphic elements, the logo should always have sufficient clear space around it to maintain visual clarity and impact.

This breathing room reinforces the logo’s presence and ensures consistent recognition across different contexts and media.

Brand Colors

The brand identity is built around a bold yet cohesive color palette. The primary color provides a strong and stable foundation, while the secondary color introduces contrast and vibrancy.

A dynamic blue gradient adds depth and a modern, energetic feel, making it ideal for highlights and key visual accents.

Triplevate Skills

The prefix “triple” represents the multiplication of creative potential through three core forces:

  • Inspiration — igniting ideas and nurturing creative vision.
  • Collaboration — amplifying creativity through partnerships and collective effort.
  • Execution — transforming concepts into tangible results through thoughtful and innovative solutions.

Outcome

The resulting identity provides Tripple We with a flexible and expressive visual system that scales across digital and physical media.

For me, this project was about translating abstract ideas — unity, growth, and momentum — into a clear, memorable visual language.

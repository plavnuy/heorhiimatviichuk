X-Plane

Project Web Design & Brand System
Scope Website, Brandbook, E-commerce
Role Web Design, Visual Identity, UI Systems
Industry Flight Simulation & Aviation

Overview

X-Plane is a professional-grade flight simulator developed over more than 30 years by aviation engineers and software specialists. Known for its physical accuracy and deep system simulation, the platform prioritizes realism over arcade-style experiences.

The project focused on translating this technical depth into a clear and modern digital presence — redesigning the public website, building a cohesive brand system, and designing an online store for aircraft models and add-ons. The challenge was to communicate engineering precision while appealing to both experienced aviation enthusiasts and newcomers.

Brand System

A comprehensive brandbook was developed to unify typography, color systems, layout principles, and visual tone across platforms.

The visual language emphasizes clarity, contrast, and spatial rhythm, echoing aviation instrumentation and cockpit interfaces.

Website Design

The website structure highlights the simulator’s key strengths: realism, long-term development, and technical credibility.

Large visuals, restrained motion, and precise typography create a sense of scale, atmosphere, and confidence.

E-commerce & Store

An integrated store was designed for aircraft models and add-ons, focusing on clarity, comparison, and technical specifications.

Product pages balance visual presentation with detailed data, supporting informed purchasing decisions for advanced users.

Outcome

The result is a cohesive digital ecosystem that reflects X-Plane’s legacy, technical excellence, and future direction.

The case demonstrates the ability to work with complex, highly specialized products while maintaining visual clarity and strong brand consistency.
